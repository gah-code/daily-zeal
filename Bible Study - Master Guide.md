### 1.2 Why handwriting and Obsidian have different jobs

The project assigns handwriting to attentive personal formulation and digital tools to organization, comparison, revision, preservation, and reuse.

**Handwriting asks:** Can I explain this without copying someone else's explanation?

**Obsidian asks:** Can I locate, connect, verify, and develop that explanation later?

**Revision asks:** Is my sentence actually true, and does it say exactly what I intend?

**Publication asks:** Can another person understand the argument without access to my private study process?

Do not let any one tool absorb the other roles. A beautifully organized vault does not replace personal reflection; a moving handwritten paragraph does not replace checking its claims.

### 1.3 What successful use produces

A fruitful session might produce an accurate paragraph, a corrected misunderstanding, an honest prayer, a practical act of mercy, or a well-formed question that remains unresolved. The project's establised aspiration is **one page by hand, one paragraph, one sentence saved**.

That is a formation rhythm-not a moral obligation, a measure of God's favor, or a demand to publish daily. On difficult days where my faculties are taken up by my work in techologies, I could could preserve the essential movment rather than manufacture length, in other words don't force my inner action out of fear of not being productive enough, more on this later.

Never let research end without producing a sentence, note, idea of my own.

## 3. How the whole formula works

### 3.1 The dependency between sections

The sections form a reasoning chain. Each answers a different question, and each constrains what follows.

| Core section | Question | What it gives the next section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Reader Question | What question am I bringing to this passage? | A focus that the text can confirm, refine, or correct. |
| 2. Main Plot | What happens, or what is argued? | A reliable overview. |
| 3. Acts / Movements | How does the passage develop? | Verse-bounded evidence and structure. |
| 4. Historical / Literal Sense | What does this text communicate in context? | Interpretive grounding. |
| 5. Theological Insight | What truth emerges from that reading? | A clear, appropriately limited claim. |
| 6. Catholic Connection | How does this relate to the Church's faith? | Canonical and doctrinal coherence. |
| 7. Virtue / Warning | What disposition or disorder does this illuminate? | A morally meaningful response. |
| 8. Prayer | How do I answer God? | Dependence, thanksgiving, repentance, or intercession. |
| 9. Practice | What will I actually do? | A concrete expression of the response. |
| 10. Writing Output | What can I explain and preserve? | A reusable piece of clear theological writing. |
